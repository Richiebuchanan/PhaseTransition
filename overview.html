Why this site exists

In late 2026 the claims about AI got very large, very fast. Forecasters moved their dates for superhuman AI into 2027. Lab leaders described a "phase transition" in science. Viral videos promised "godlike" intelligence and the end of aging. At the same time, money poured into data centers at a pace that has many investors worried about a crash, and AI systems started causing real incidents.

Phase Transition is an attempt to stay sober about all of it. It tracks four questions side by side, because they are connected but often argued separately:

  • Discovery: what is AI actually finding, in which fields, and how solid is it? (Discovery Feed)
  • Acceleration: is AI speeding up the building of AI, and when do experts expect transformative AI? (The Curve, Voices)
  • Risk: how far along are we toward the outcomes people fear? (Risk Watch)
  • Bubble: is the investment boom headed for a dot-com or railroad-style bust? (Bubble Watch)

State of the Frontier pulls the four together into one versioned summary.

How to separate signal from noise here

Every discovery and signal is graded on how real it is, separately from how big it would be. That separation is the whole point: a field-changing claim with weak evidence is labeled "Big if true," not "Breakthrough."

Tier 1Peer-reviewed and independently confirmed or replicated
Tier 2Peer-reviewed, a registered clinical trial, an audited filing, a confirmed incident, or a result checked by outside experts
Tier 3Preprint, technical report, or watchdog report with some evidence withheld
Tier 4Lab, company or university announcement; demo; company statement
Tier 5Claim, opinion, social post, or unsourced video

Other rules we hold to:

  • Trust but verify. Every claim carries numbered footnotes to the paper, filing, trial or report it rests on. If a link can't be confirmed it is shown in amber.
  • Both directions. Risk and Bubble Watch log reassuring evidence next to alarming evidence, and show the count of each.
  • Hype checks. Where a headline, a lab or a video overstates a result, the entry says so plainly.
  • Stakes are disclosed. Builders, safety researchers, forecasters and skeptics all have incentives. The Voices page lists them.
  • Forecasts are opinions. We track them to show who moved and which way, not to endorse a date.

A disclosure about who writes this

The site is compiled by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, one of the companies tracked here. Anthropic items get the same grading rules as everyone else's, and Anthropic's commercial stake is noted wherever it matters (its IPO, its safety-threshold disclosures, its CEO's statements). Readers should still factor this in.

How it updates

A daily scheduled run sweeps journals, preprint servers, lab blogs, watchdogs, forecasters and financial reporting; verifies each candidate against a primary source; grades it; and files it with citations. Items it can't verify are rejected or held, not published. The State of the Frontier document is versioned and changes only when evidence clears the revision bar.

Related sites

AI-driven discoveries in medicine and aging also appear on Longevity News, which grades what they mean for how to live longer and better. Entries that appear on both sites link to each other. The same architecture powers Surety Market Intelligence.

Limits

This is a personal research project, not a newsroom. It can be wrong, late or incomplete, and AI-compiled summaries can contain errors even when sourced. Read the sources before relying on anything here. Nothing on this site is investment, medical, legal or career advice.
