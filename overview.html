Why this site exists

In late 2026, the claims about AI got very big, very fast. Experts moved their predictions for "smarter-than-human" AI to as early as 2027. Company leaders talked about a turning point in science. Viral videos promised "godlike" machines and the end of aging. Meanwhile, money poured into AI so fast that many investors worry about a crash, and AI systems started causing real problems.

Phase Transition tries to stay calm and clear-headed about all of it. A "phase transition" is when something changes state all at once, like water freezing into ice. Some people think AI is about to do that to the world. This site tracks the evidence for and against, in five parts:

  • The record: what has happened since January 2023, month by month, with a check for gaps. (Timeline for the one-line version, The Record for the full detail)
  • Discoveries: what is AI actually finding, and how sure are we? (Discovery Feed)
  • Speed: is AI helping build better AI, and when do experts think it will match humans? (The Curve, Voices & Players)
  • The upside: what good is AI doing, and what's only promised? (What Could Go Right)
  • The risks: how close are we to the bad outcomes people fear? (What Could Go Wrong)
  • The money: is the spending a bubble that could crash? (Bubble Watch)

State of the Frontier pulls it all together on one page.

How we separate real news from hype

Every item gets two separate grades: how solid the evidence is and how big a deal it would be. That's the whole trick. A huge claim with weak evidence gets labeled "Big if true," not "Breakthrough." It's like a weather report: one number says how sure the forecaster is, another says how big the storm would be.

Tier 1Confirmed: checked by independent experts and repeated
Tier 2Checked by experts: published after expert review, tested in a fair trial, an official filing, or a confirmed event
Tier 3Early report: shared publicly but not yet reviewed, or a report with some evidence kept private
Tier 4Company says: an announcement or demo from the people who made it
Tier 5Opinion or claim: a social post, an interview, or a video without sources

A few more rules we stick to:

  • Show your work. The little numbers next to claims link to the original source, so you can check it yourself.
  • Good news and bad news. The upside and the risks get equal space, and each page logs evidence in both directions.
  • Reality checks. When a headline, a company or a video oversells something, we say so plainly.
  • Everyone has a stake. People building AI, safety experts and skeptics all have reasons to spin. The Voices & Players page lists them, for people and companies alike.
  • Predictions are guesses. We track them to show who changed their mind, not to tell you what will happen.
  • Plain English. If a word needs explaining, it has a dotted underline. Hover over it or tap it.

Who writes this

The site is put together by Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, which is one of the companies tracked here. We grade Anthropic by the same rules as everyone else and point out its stake wherever it matters. You should still keep that in mind.

How it updates

Every day, an automatic check looks through science journals, research sites, company announcements, safety testers, forecasters and financial news. Each new item is checked against its original source, graded and added with citations. Anything that can't be checked is left out. The State of the Frontier page only changes when strong new evidence arrives, and old versions are kept so you can see what changed.

Limits

This is a personal research project, not a news organization. It can be wrong, late or missing things, and AI-written summaries can contain mistakes even when they cite sources. Check the sources before relying on anything here. Nothing on this site is financial, medical, legal or career advice.
