How long a task can AI agents finish on their own?

METR's "time horizon": the length of software, machine-learning and cyber task (measured in skilled-human hours) that a model completes 50% of the time. It is the most-cited capability trend and the input to most forecasts, but it has known weaknesses: 50% is not reliable, the task suite is running out of headroom above about 16 hours, and analysis choices can move recent estimates by up to 35%.
