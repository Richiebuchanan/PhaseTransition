What this page is. A month-by-month record of what happened in AI since January 1, 2023, the weeks after ChatGPT arrived and many people first sensed something big was coming. New models, real discoveries, things going right, warning signs, money, laws and people. Each entry is graded like the rest of the site and linked to its source.

Want the quick version? The Timeline page shows every story in one sentence on a single interactive timeline.

Think of it like a ship's logbook. One line at a time it looks routine. Read straight through, you can see the ship has turned.
